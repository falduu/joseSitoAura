Blanco José | Executive Chef

A passion for cooking, attention to detail, and the pursuit of excellence are the ingredients behind every creation.

I am Blanco José, Executive Chef at Aura Catering, with over 20 years of experience in the restaurant and fine-dining industry. Throughout my career, I have developed expertise in Italian and international cuisine, bespoke menu design, and the planning of exclusive events, receptions, and catering services.

My goal is to turn every occasion into a unique dining experience, bringing together quality ingredients, creativity, elegance, and professionalism to create unforgettable moments.

At Aura Catering, every event is delivered with passion, precision, and a bespoke vision. I believe true excellence is not only what is served at the table, but the feeling guests take with them.
