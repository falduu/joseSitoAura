AURA CATERING — SEO AUDIT BRIEF

Objective
Review the existing static website and prepare a practical, evidence-based SEO
proposal. This is an audit and recommendation task first, not authorization for
wide-ranging site changes.

Important working rules
- Inspect the current repository and deployed-site configuration before making
  assumptions. The working tree may contain intentional changes; preserve them.
- Keep the site static and compatible with GitHub Pages. Do not add PHP, a
  database, a server-side form, frameworks, dependencies, or build tooling.
- Do not remove, replace, or restructure existing pages, navigation, team
  content, galleries, or other sections as part of the SEO audit.
- Do not change brand copy, design, contact details, or SEO metadata without
  first presenting the proposed changes and receiving approval.
- Do not invent services, locations, credentials, customer claims, company
  details, or business facts.
- Keep auracatering.it as the intended canonical domain. auracatering.eu is
  intended to redirect to auracatering.it; verify the hosting/provider setup
  rather than assuming a repository file can configure that redirect.
- The current site is a static showcase. Do not add a contact form. Contact
  details may be displayed as static links on the existing contacts page.

Audit areas
1. Inventory the actual HTML pages and identify the purpose of each page.
2. Check indexability, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical URLs, redirects,
   duplicate pages, and consistency of the auracatering.it domain.
3. Review page titles, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, language
   declarations, internal links, image alt text, and structured data.
4. Review mobile usability, accessibility basics, image sizes, and performance
   opportunities relevant to a static GitHub Pages site.
5. Identify local SEO opportunities only after confirming the real service
   area, business address or service-area status, and appropriate business
   profile details.
6. Recommend a measurement and launch checklist, including Google Search
   Console and sitemap submission, without claiming ranking guarantees.

Information that must be confirmed with the owner before tailoring keywords or
publishing location/business claims
- Priority catering and hospitality services.
- Ideal customers and events.
- Actual locations and service area.
- Desired website languages and priority language.
- Legal company name and business details when available.
- Whether auracatering.it is live and whether auracatering.eu redirects to it.

Required output
- First return a concise audit with findings, evidence (file and line), severity
  or priority, and recommended changes.
- Separate facts verified from assumptions and unanswered owner questions.
- Do not implement site changes during the audit. Wait for the owner to approve
  a specific set of changes before editing files.

Future content note
Once the legal company is created, update the footer legal/company information
only with the verified company name and details supplied by the owner.
