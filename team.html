What we stand by

The standards we race by

Four principles, pinned above every pass — kitchen, bar and floor.

01

Craft

Everything by hand, from the bread to the ice. Machines help; hands decide.

02

Precision

Motorsport tolerances, applied to the table: timings to the minute, set-ups to the millimetre, service to the second.

03

Discretion

Who sits at our table stays at our table. Privacy is the one course we always serve.

04

Warmth

Impeccable, never cold. The Italian way: precise in the details, easy in the air.
